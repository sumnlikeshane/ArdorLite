# Evidence and publication policy

The preparation observed the working tree on 11 October 2026, including modified and untracked files. Public provenance uses source-relative labels and actual file hashes. Absolute source roots, branch/remote details, selected-file working-tree state and private inventory receipts remain outside this package.

The export is an explicit per-file allowlist. Publication inventory decisions apply to the selected derivative, not to the complete original or to all aggregates. Included analytical summaries express archived findings without price panels, ticker/date rows, identities, account amounts or private operational records. Rights remain reserved. Full restricted originals are excluded even where an authored summary describes their outcome.

Every derivative records its source IDs, original hashes, export hash, extraction/redaction rules, claims, maturity and publication decision. Each chart has an included machine-readable input, accessible table and specification. No new return calculation or strategy inference is used. Missing results stay null; forecasts are labelled forecasts; complete and partial folds remain separate.

Reproduction levels are executable_example, archived_result, report_transcription and private_inputs_required. An independently authored synthetic example demonstrates a method without certifying its source strategy. Historical return replication requires excluded private inputs. Original reports and row artifacts are not supplied.

The main ledgers have historical seal discrepancies and are audit history, not current certification authority. They have not been repaired, restored or resealed. Atlas's accepted map has no formal nominations; its invalid predecessor and separate executable follow-up retain separate identities. Source-rights outcomes are dated archived governance findings, not fresh legal conclusions.

Default exclusions include credentials/config, raw/vendor data, databases and caches, protected ledgers/backups, row-level trades/exceptions, private snapshots/holdouts, personal amounts/holdings/trade plans, complete internal decision packets, and unreviewed upstream code/assets. These categories appear in the exclusion receipt; none may be admitted by a recursive copy.
