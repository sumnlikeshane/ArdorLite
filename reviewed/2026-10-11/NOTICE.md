# Attribution and treatment

Prepared from the Ardor working-tree research observed on 11 October 2026. Each selected original is identified by its repository-relative path and actual SHA-256 in evidence/manifest.json. Narrative and chart inputs are sanitized derivatives, not complete originals. Numerical source outputs are historical research evidence; synthetic examples are software demonstrations.

The source project's pyproject.toml declares its licence Proprietary. Attribution is retained at project level; no biography or individual contribution claim is supplied. No open-source licence is inferred or granted by this package. Included newly authored summaries, rendering/validation tools and independent examples retain reserved rights pending the destination's ownership/licence choice.

No source engines, TradeX-derived ports, Pine scripts, vendor datasets, third-party images or complete internal decision packets are redistributed. The mathematical method demonstrations are independently written and document their conceptual source references and differences. Matplotlib is an optional external dependency used to render new figures; its code is not bundled. Its upstream licence remains applicable when installed.

Original source rights restrictions remain unresolved for the affected data. The bridge study's archived numerical repair and its failed source-rights gate are separate outcomes. This package includes aggregate analytical and QA conclusions only; it does not confer collection, use or redistribution rights to original prices or rows.
